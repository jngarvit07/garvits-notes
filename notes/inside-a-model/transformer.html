The Transformer, assembled

2 min read Read the AI note first
💡
In plain English
One attention step is like one pass of editing: each word reconsiders itself in light of the others. Do it a hundred times, with a small amount of processing between each pass, and something remarkable happens — early passes settle grammar and word sense, middle passes assemble facts and relationships, late passes decide what to actually say. Nobody assigned those jobs. They emerged.
PieceWhat it doesWhy it is there
Positional informationTells each token where it sitsAttention alone is order-blind. Without this, “dog bites man” and “man bites dog” are identical inputs.
Residual connectionAdds each layer's output to its input rather than replacing itInformation can travel a hundred layers without being degraded. Deep networks did not train reliably until this existed.
NormalisationRescales the numbers between stepsKeeps values from exploding or vanishing across many layers. Pure plumbing, and training fails without it.
Feed-forwardProcesses each position independentlyRoughly two thirds of the parameters. The evidence suggests this is where factual knowledge is retained.
i
What “70 billion parameters” means. A parameter is one number, learned during training, that shapes how signal flows through the network. Seventy billion of them is the model. They are fixed once training ends — running the model does not change them, which is why a model cannot learn from your conversation. Anything that looks like memory is text being fed back in, not the model changing.
