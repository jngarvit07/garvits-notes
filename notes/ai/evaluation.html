Evaluation: what turns this into engineering

2 min read No experience needed

Every team building with models goes through the same arc: a prompt that works, a change that seems better, a slow discovery that something else broke, and finally the realisation that nobody can tell whether the system is improving. Evaluation is what ends that.

💡
In plain English
A test suite for something that never gives the same answer twice. You cannot assert equality, so you assert properties: did it find the right document, did it stay inside the sources, did it refuse when it should, is it under the length limit.
What to measureHowCatches
Retrieval qualityIs the correct chunk in the top five, for known questions?The commonest cause of bad RAG answers.
GroundingDoes every claim trace to a retrieved source?Confident invention that reads perfectly.
Refusal behaviourAsk things the corpus cannot answer.A system that would rather guess than say no.
Format validityDoes the output parse against the schema?The failure that breaks code rather than annoying a reader.
SafetyInjection attempts, hostile inputs, out-of-scope requests.Regressions in the defences the security chapter describes.
Cost and latencyTokens and seconds per answer, per version.A quality improvement that quietly tripled the bill.
i
Log real failures back into the set. The suite should grow every time somebody reports something odd. A year in, that file — not the prompt — is the asset.
